# Integrated Pointercrate

Brings the [Pointercrate](https://pointercrate.com) Demon List directly into Geometry Dash.

## Features

- **Demon List**: browse the official Pointercrate demon list (position, name, verifier, publisher, requirement), 10 demons per page, paginated end-to-end via cursor pagination until the list is exhausted.
- **Player Ranking**: browse the global Pointercrate leaderboard 10 players per page, search your own name to see your rank and score, and have it highlighted in the list.

Two buttons are added to the main menu to open the Demon List and Player Ranking screens. No configuration needed.

This mod is not affiliated with Pointercrate.
